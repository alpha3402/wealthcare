WealthCard & Mastercard

The WealthCard is your gateway to a smarter way of spending.

Powered by Mastercard’s trusted global payments network, the WealthCard transforms everyday purchases into opportunities to receive real financial value.

How it works → Find offers

The model

Rewards on purchases you were already making.

When cardholders shop with participating WealthShare merchants, those businesses can reward successful transactions with cashback or other financial benefits. Instead of relying on traditional advertising that may never result in a sale, merchants invest directly in customers who have already chosen to buy.

Pay as normal

No change in shopping habits — simply pay as you normally would wherever Mastercard is accepted.

Shop participating businesses

When you purchase from participating WealthShare merchants, your transaction qualifies for rewards.

Rewards applied automatically

Eligible rewards are applied automatically — no forms, no claims, no effort.

The partnership

Mastercard powers the payment. WealthShare unlocks the value.

Mastercard provides the secure, globally accepted payment infrastructure, while WealthShare delivers the intelligence, merchant partnerships and value-sharing platform that connects businesses with consumers.

Together, they combine world-class payment technology with a new model of commerce — one where every transaction has the potential to create value for both businesses and consumers.
