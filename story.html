Our story

Commerce has always been built around one simple truth: customers create value.

Every purchase fuels businesses, strengthens communities and drives economic growth. Yet the people who create that value have traditionally received very little in return — while businesses continue to spend billions on marketing that often fails to deliver measurable results.

A better system

We believe it’s time for a better system.

WealthShare exists to redefine the relationship between consumers and businesses by transforming every transaction into shared value.

Our platform connects motivated consumers with businesses that want measurable growth. Instead of paying for advertising that may never produce a sale, merchants reward successful transactions — creating a model where everyone wins.

Consumers

Receive effortless financial rewards from their everyday spending.

Businesses

Only pay when they achieve genuine customer outcomes.

Communities

Benefit as local spending becomes stronger, smarter and more sustainable.

More than cashback

It is the evolution of commerce.

This is more than cashback. We call it Consumer Value Monetisation — unlocking the financial value hidden within everyday transactions and returning it to the people who create it.

Our ambition
